Social Video Studio by Uncle Dolo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Summary

Social Video Studio is a personal desktop tool used by Uncle Dolo (TikTok: @dologrove) to upload his own videos to his own accounts. It does not collect, sell, rent, or share anyone's personal data.

2. What data the app uses

  • Basic profile information from the connected TikTok account (such as display name, username, and profile picture), used only to show which account is signed in.
  • The videos and captions you choose to upload, which are sent directly to the platform you pick.
  • Sign-in tokens issued by TikTok and other platforms, which let the app post on your behalf.

The app does not use analytics, ads, or tracking, and it does not access data from any other TikTok users.

3. Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own PC, in Windows Credential Manager. Videos stay on that PC until you upload them. Nothing is stored on any server run by Uncle Dolo, because there isn't one.

4. Sharing

Data is not shared with anyone. The only place it goes is the platform you choose to post to (for example TikTok or YouTube), and only when you click to upload. That platform's own privacy policy then applies.

5. Revoking access

You can disconnect the app at any time. In the TikTok app, go to Profile, then Menu, then Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions (or Apps and services permissions), find Social Video Studio, and remove access. You can also sign out of the account inside Social Video Studio.

6. Deleting your data

Signing out in the app, or deleting the saved sign-in from Windows Credential Manager, removes the tokens from the PC. Uninstalling the app removes its local files. Content you already posted can be deleted on the platform itself. You can also email the contact below to ask for deletion.

7. Children

The app is not meant for children and does not knowingly handle data from anyone under 13.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
